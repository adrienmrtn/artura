Artura gives you one masterpiece a day and the story behind it, in just a few minutes. No degree, no jargon. Just a curious eye and a few minutes.

ONE ARTWORK EVERY DAY
Scroll a vertical feed of great paintings, filter by movement or artist, search, and save your favorites to your library.

LEARN TO REALLY LOOK
Short lessons are read one block at a time. The camera zooms into the painting and points out the details that make it great, from brushwork and light to hidden symbols. Then a quick quiz helps it stick.

REMEMBER WHAT YOU LEARN
Review brings back cards from the lessons you have read, a little every day, so names, dates and ideas stay with you. Want more? Practice freely whenever you like.

GAMES THAT TRAIN YOUR EYE

• Palette: spot the colors behind the masterpiece
