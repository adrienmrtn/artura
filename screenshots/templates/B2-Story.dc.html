The Starry Night
Vincent van Gogh · 1889 · MoMA, New York

June 1889. Van Gogh is 36. A month earlier, he checked himself into the asylum of Saint-Rémy-de-Provence.

Did you know?
Van Gogh only started painting at 27.

He painted this sky by day, from memory and imagination.
